# Cognitive architecture literature manifest

The PDF files are intentionally excluded from Git. This manifest preserves the
expected filenames, bibliographic records, retrieval sources, and page counts;
[`SHA256SUMS`](SHA256SUMS) records the integrity hash of each validated local
copy. After obtaining the papers, verify the corpus from this directory with
`sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.

Collected 2026-08-07 and expanded 2026-08-08. All 30 requested records were
validated as parseable PDFs. Numbered filenames retain the reading-list order;
records #28–#30 were supplied by the user and normalized to canonical filenames
without being downloaded again.

## Retrieval failures and substitutions

- #1: the CCRG host had an incomplete TLS certificate chain. The author manuscript downloaded only after narrowly bypassing certificate verification for that URL; its DOI and title were then validated locally.
- #12: the guessed PMC PDF endpoint returned an HTML interstitial under a `.pdf` filename. It was discarded and replaced with the matching 23-page PDF from the University of Giessen archive.
- #15: a guessed publisher path returned 404; ResearchGate and ScienceDirect returned 403; OpenAlex full-text returned 401. The matching 13-page CC BY paper was retrieved from Chapman University's author repository.
- #26: MDPI returned 403 for its publisher PDF, and the NCBI open-access API pointed to an archive that returned 404. The matching author manuscript was retrieved from arXiv.
- #27: the publisher version was paywalled. The matching title/authors were retrieved as the September 1987 AIP-9 technical-report version from Carnegie Mellon University's repository.
- Final unresolved failures: none.
