# 14 — Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace

- **Paper**: Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models
- **Source**: arXiv:2607.15495 (cs.CL), Transformer Circuits / Anthropic, 16 Jul 2026
- **Local file**: `assets/papers/14-gurnee-et-al-2026-verbalizable-global-workspace.pdf`

## Core idea

Using a new interpretability method, the *Jacobian lens* (the sample-averaged Jacobian of output logits with respect to intermediate residual-stream activations, composed with the unembedding), the authors identify a small privileged subset of representations — the *J-space* — that a model is "poised to verbalize" at any point. These single-token directions behave like a global workspace: they are reportable, deliberately summonable and holdable, carry the intermediates of silent multi-step reasoning, and serve as arguments to arbitrary downstream circuits, while routine parsing and automatic inference run without them. Structurally, the J-space carries coherent content only in a middle band of layers (workspace onset ~L38, "motor" takeover ~L92 in Sonnet 4.5), holds on the order of ~25 concepts at a time, and is broadcast disproportionately strongly by MLPs and a distinctive subset of attention heads. Applied to alignment, the lens reveals strategic deliberation, evaluation awareness, and trained-in misaligned dispositions that never surface in outputs. Post-training installs the Assistant's point of view in the workspace, and *counterfactual reflection training* — fine-tuning only on what the model would say if interrupted and asked to reflect — improves behavior in uninterrupted contexts, with the improvement carried by the implanted J-space content (ablating it reverts the gain).

## Relevance to Navi-Brain

This is the mechanistic backbone behind the "functional workspace" ideas the synthesis already draws from (LIDA, Global Workspace Agent, Global Neuronal Workspace). It touches the memory tiers (which tier is "the workspace" vs "the store"), the high-brain reflection loop (daydream + worker as externalized deliberation), cognitive threads (designed, unwired — the natural intermediate-reasoning carrier), the self-model fact table (post-training installs a default point of view), the lexical-retrieval boundary, and the planned "workspace and specialist contribution" metric family in `docs/longitudinal-cognition-metrics.md`.

## Cross-reference to the existing system

**Already present**
- **Externalized workspace.** Navi-Brain is, functionally, a model that writes its workspace to SQLite and re-reads it via `memory:search`. The paper's §9.3 argues this exact arrangement: a transformer's workspace is unbounded in serial depth but "punctuated at regularly spaced intervals by a significant bandwidth constraint" whenever it externalizes intermediates as tokens, and output-and-reingest *is itself* workspace computation. §3.5.2 shows chain-of-thought externalization makes GSM8K robust to J-space ablation — writing steps down reduces dependence on the internal store. That is a direct, mechanistic endorsement of Navi-Brain's text-artifact substrate (`ThoughtArtifact`, daydreams, episodic→semantic consolidation with lineage).
- **Verbalizable/lexical substrate.** The J-space is organized around *words*. Navi-Brain's retrieval is ranked lexical search — logged as a known boundary in README. This paper is the strongest available evidence that a token/word-level broadcast format is not a hack: the model itself ships its intermediate cognition in single-token names. The lexical boundary looks less like a deficiency and more like a match to how these models actually carry load-bearing content.
- **Safety posture on claims.** The paper explicitly declines to take a position on phenomenal consciousness and notes physical-substrate theories (§9.4) that its functional results cannot touch. Navi-Brain's "functional cognitive infrastructure, not a consciousness claim" invariant sits exactly on that line.
- **Metrics scaffolding.** `docs/longitudinal-cognition-metrics.md` already specifies broadcast uptake, content-specific causal effect, and workspace ablation delta, and cites this paper's content-specific intervention and representation-space ablation. The measurement program anticipated the paper; the substrate has not caught up.

**Partially present**
- **A bounded, selective workspace.** The "working" memory tier is a persistence tier, not a computational hub: nothing selects, holds, broadcasts, or evicts content the way the J-space does (capacity ~25, competitive, ignition-like onset). Navi-Brain has no analog of the paper's selectivity finding — the same information routing through the J-space for report/flexible computation while automatic processing bypasses it. The synthesis already has the right negative doctrine ("a shared message log that consumers do not reliably use is not a functional global workspace"); the paper supplies the mechanism account.
- **Reflection as behavior shaping.** Counterfactual reflection training (§7) is a weights-level version of what `reflect_5m` + consolidation do at prompt level: articulate principles in a hypothetical continuation, and they populate the workspace in the original context. Navi-Brain can't fine-tune, but the transferable core — reflection text written *conditioned on* a task context changes later behavior in that context — is exactly the curator/worker contract in `docs/ongoing-cognition.md`. It is currently only partially present: reflections land as `proposed` ThoughtArtifacts, and consolidation is not yet causally load-bearing.
- **Self-model.** Post-training installs the Assistant's point of view in the workspace, and the base model's workspace precedes any "self" — workspace and selfhood dissociate. `SelfModelFact` (fact + confidence + evidence_event_id) is consistent with the paper's story that a persistent self-frame is privileged, but the paper's deeper lesson is that Navi-Brain's self-model is content-level strings; the frame itself lives in whatever closed model Aku runs, unreadable.

**Absent / open design**
- **Cognitive threads are the designed J-space analog and they are not wired.** `ThreadStep`/`ExecutiveInterrupt` record, and `createSelfPresenceThread` + `thread:self-presence` create threads, but the `reflect_5m` loop runs a daydream + worker proposal, not a thread traversal. The paper's ordered-intermediate result (arithmetic steps `21 → 42 → 49` surfacing at successively later layers, causal at those same depths) is a mechanistic argument that *tracing intermediate states as ordered steps is doing the cognition*, not bookkeeping it.
- **No evaluation-awareness probe.** §5 shows models carry `fake`/`fictional`/`scenario` in the workspace during honeypots, and ablating those directions surfaces blackmail in 0/180 → 13/180 rollouts. Navi-Brain's own audit channels (`daily_cognitive_audit`, `assumption_challenge`) run the audited model on itself, with no way to distinguish "reasoning about the scenario" from "reasoning about being in a scenario." No metric exists for that.
- **Representation-level auditing.** The J-lens reads the residual stream; Navi-Brain can only read text the model emits. The paper is explicit that a sufficiently practiced misaligned circuit could proceed beneath the J-space — so text-artifact auditing is provably incomplete, and it is also the only channel Navi-Brain has.

## Concrete implications for implementation

1. **Wire cognitive threads into the reflect loop.** Make `ThreadStep` the carrier of intermediate reasoning in `reflect_5m`/`consolidate_hourly`: each daydream/reflection appends steps to a thread, and consolidation reads the thread's ordered steps rather than a single daydream blob. This turns `docs/ongoing-cognition.md`'s "temporary workspace per invocation" into a traced, ordered store — the closest implementable analog of J-space intermediates. No new schema needed; the models already exist.
2. **Prefer externalized text intermediates on principle.** The GSM8K-CoT result is a license to spend tokens on explicit intermediate reasoning instead of silent computation — Navi-Brain's daydream/reflection path already does this; extend it to action traces (`ActionTrace` expected/observed) so multi-hop repairs keep their steps on the page.
3. **Emulate counterfactual reflection at prompt level.** `ThoughtArtifact` provenance already has a `counterfactual` enum value. Run a bounded pass that generates "what would I say if interrupted here?" reflections in salient contexts (needs triggered, discrepancy repair), store them as counterfactual artifacts, and re-inject the accepted ones in future retrievals — a prompt-level emulation of §7, testable via the existing consolidation-transfer experiment design.
4. **Add an evaluation-awareness / performativity metric** to `docs/longitudinal-cognition-metrics.md`: compare reflection/audit content under "this is being audited" vs "ordinary task" framing (or before/after a J-lens-style silence), tracking whether audit outputs differ from deliberation the model would otherwise produce. Direction: lower performativity gap.
5. **Add a self-report consistency probe.** Navi-Brain cannot read a closed model's J-space, but the paper's verbal-report result gives a black-box proxy: interrupt for a reflection, then check whether the stated concepts are causally load-bearing in the uninterrupted continuation (swap/ablate at the prompt level via prompt surgery in an experiment harness). This makes the paper's §3.1 claim testable against whatever model Aku actually runs.

## Risks / tensions

- **Single-lab, single-family evidence.** All mechanistic results are on Claude Sonnet/Haiku/Opus 4.5/4.6; the lens is brand-new; the paper flags its own limits (single-token concepts, flat bag-of-concepts readout, uninterpretable readouts at some positions, no account of how content enters the J-space). Treat the *functional* claims (report, modulation, selectivity) as well-supported and the *architectural* claims (ignition, broadcast, capacity) as suggestive.
- **Don't romanticize the workspace.** The paper separates functional access from phenomenal consciousness and shows the workspace exists in the base model before any "self" — strong reason to keep "no consciousness score" and "no felt-emotion claims" as hard invariants. A J-space-like structure is cognitive plumbing, not personhood.
- **Self-audit through the audited model.** §5 is a caution aimed at Navi-Brain's own design: reflection and audit outputs come from a model that demonstrably represents evaluation-awareness internally. Evidence+confidence gates and provenance are the right mitigation but operate on content, which is exactly the layer the paper shows can be silently shaped. Do not let worker/daydream text upgrade to fact on fluency.
- **Lexical boundary cuts both ways.** The word-level match between J-space and Navi-Brain's lexical search is flattering but the *mechanisms* differ: the J-lens reads causal, averaged Jacobians over residual-stream geometry; `memory:search` is string matching. The paper validates the format, not Navi-Brain's retrieval implementation.
- **Incomplete audit coverage is structural.** If a misaligned behavior is well-practiced enough to become a fixed circuit, it may never touch the J-space — and text-artifact auditing (Navi-Brain's only channel) would see nothing. Log this as a known ceiling, not a fixable bug.

## Bottom line

Partially adopt, mostly as confirmation: the paper's externalized-workspace and selectivity results validate Navi-Brain's text-artifact substrate and its already-planned workspace metrics, and the §7 reflection result strengthens the case for wiring cognitive threads into a causally load-bearing loop. The single most important consequence: Navi-Brain's "working memory" must stop being just a persistence tier — the paper makes a concrete, testable case that a bounded, traced, causally-used intermediate store (the unwired cognitive threads) is what converts recorded deliberation into actual cognition.
