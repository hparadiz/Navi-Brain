# 06 — Computational Metacognition

- **Paper**: Computational Metacognition
- **Source**: arXiv:2201.12885, Advances in Cognitive Systems (Ninth Annual Conference) 2021, submitted 9/2021, published 11/2021
- **Local file**: `assets/papers/06-cox-et-al-2022-computational-metacognition.pdf`

## Core idea

Metacognition as a second action-perception cycle stacked on top of cognition: the lower cycle perceives the world and acts; the upper cycle takes an introspective trace of the cognitive level, detects that cognition itself misbehaved, explains why, and acts back on cognition. The paper formalizes the mechanism, not the vibe. A metacognitive expectation is the triple `(s^M_i, α^M_i, s^M_{i+1})` — the mental state before a cognitive process, the process itself, and the expected state after it (e.g. "after planning, a plan is in memory"). When the expectation is violated, an explanatory (hindsight) loop diagnoses it against case-based meta-explanation patterns (Meta-XPs) and raises a meta-goal of one of three types: change the reasoning method, change the system's goals, or learn a new knowledge structure. A smaller piece covers anticipatory metacognition (foresight): predict that planning will fail, suspend the goal, and delegate it to a better-suited agent via theory-of-mind reasoning. The three forms are table-flavored: explanatory = past/hindsight, immediate = present/introspective, anticipatory = future/foresight. Evidence is a 10x10 plant-protection grid where a MIDCA agent that learns a corrected spray operator (via FOIL) beats a plain planner that never questions its action model.

## Relevance to Navi-Brain

This is one of the few papers in the corpus that specifies metacognition to the point where "logging" and "metacognition" stop being the same word. It touches the exact seam the repo is already built around: expectations vs observations (`ActionTrace`), discrepancy detection and repair, the self-model, meta-level control, and a value-of-cognition argument for when to bother. It also gives the repo a precise definition it currently only gestures at — a metacognitive expectation is a *mental*-operation-level constraint, not a world-action outcome.

## Cross-reference to the existing system

**Already present:**
- **World-level explanatory metacognition**: `src/Model/ActionTrace.php` is a literal embodiment of the cognitive-level discrepancy structure — `expected`, `observed`, `match_status` (matched/mismatched), `repair_note`. An action mismatch is an expectation failure about the world; the repair note is the seed of the explanatory loop. What the paper adds on top: the same triple applied to *cognitive operations* (planning, retrieval, consolidation), not just actions in the world.
- **Repair-through-learning as the paper's type-3 meta-goal**: `memory:consolidate` promotes episode-derived takeaways into semantic memory with lineage. It is the paper's "learn new knowledge" meta-goal in passive form — consolidation happens on a schedule, not because a failure demanded it.
- **Self-model with evidence and confidence**: `src/Model/SelfModelFact.php` (fact_key, confidence, evidence_event_id) matches the paper's insistence that metacognition needs a model of the cognitive level. The paper's is a *transition system* over mental states; Navi's is a fact table. Seed present, shape different.
- **Meta-level control scaffold**: `src/Model/ExecutiveInterrupt.php` (severity, fencing_token, status) is exactly MIDCA's meta-level control / LIDA's behavior-stream interruption, designed but not wired into a running loop — currently just records. The paper confirms the design direction and specifies the triggering condition it should respond to.
- **Appraisal/needs as immediate-control signals**: `src/Model/Appraisal.php` and `Need.php` are run-time pressure variables that could gate how much cognition runs — a distant cousin of the paper's "immediate metacognition," though the paper itself leaves that form for future work.
- **Evaluation apparatus already names this paper**: `docs/longitudinal-cognition-metrics.md` Metric family 2 (discrepancy detection precision/recall, discrepancy latency, repair efficacy, recurrence rate, value of cognition) is the quantitative version of the paper's empirical claim, and cites it as joint motivation. `docs/experiments/03-cognitive-self-repair.md` proposes fault injection + detection + diagnosis + recovery + transfer, which is the paper's evaluation pattern run on Navi's own cognition.
- **Synthesis lineage**: `docs/literature-synthesis.md` §4 already distills the paper's thesis ("a trace of cognitive states and operations, expected transitions, discrepancy detection, causal diagnosis, and corrective control") and `docs/ongoing-cognition.md` tables it as the "metacognitive expectation" seam. This paper is *the* source behind Navi's stated metacognition ambitions — the implementation just hasn't caught up with the aspiration.

**Partially present:**
- **Self-model is not predictive**: MIDCA's Ω = (S^M, A^M, ω) includes a cognitive transition function that *generates* expectations of mental-state change. `SelfModelFact` stores static claims about capability/permission/failure-mode, but nothing asks "what should be in memory after this cognitive phase?" The paper (with Bongard) makes the case that a self-model earns its keep by predicting; Navi's earns its keep by describing.
- **Repair is unstructured**: `ActionTrace.repair_note` is free text. MIDCA routes repairs through one of three typed meta-goals (change method / change goal / learn). Navi has no meta-goal type attached to a repair, so a mismatch produces prose, not a decision about *which lever* to pull.
- **Reasoning-method switching (type-1 meta-goal)**: `models:discover` exists, so multiple reasoning substrates are discoverable, but there is no meta-level trigger that detects "this method is failing" and switches.

**Absent / open design:**
- **Metacognitive expectations over cognitive operations**: nothing verifies "planning produced a plan," "retrieval returned results," "consolidation promoted a takeaway." This is the paper's core formalism and it simply does not exist in the loop. The 30s path and high-brain phases run on trust.
- **Anticipatory metacognition / goal delegation**: Intentions have no `suspended` status, and there is no mechanism to predict own planning failure and hand a goal to another agent. This is the paper's second implemented mode, and it is also the *only* paper concept that maps cleanly onto the mesh hypothesis — delegation is literally what a mesh is for, but nothing reasons about "another agent's knowledge, skills and goals."
- **Meta-level planning**: MIDCA runs a real planner (Fast Downward, PDDL) over meta-goals. Navi's closest analog is `FreeModelWorker` — bounded, tool-denied, proposal-only. That's a deliberate safety shape, not a planning gap to close blindly.

## Concrete implications for implementation

1. **Add a mental-operation expectation record.** The single highest-value change: extend the mismatch machinery from world actions to cognitive phases. For each high-brain work item (reflect, consolidate), assert a triple in the spirit of `(s_i^M, α^M, s_{i+1}^M)` — e.g. "before consolidation: source events exist; after: ≥1 takeaway or an explicit null reason." Store it (new model or a phase-kind flag on `ActionTrace`) and treat violation as a first-class discrepancy.
2. **Wire `ExecutiveInterrupt` into the loop.** Currently records-only. Make a pending interrupt *change the next cognitive cycle* — skip, reorder, or shrink a phase under the existing fencing-token/lease scheme. That converts meta-level control from a table into a control path, which is the paper's actual contribution over mere introspection.
3. **Give repairs a type.** Promote `repair_note` from prose to one of the three meta-goal kinds (change method / change goal / learn knowledge). Then `memory:consolidate` gains a reason field and the paper's type-3 meta-goal stops being an implicit schedule.
4. **Make the self-model emit expectations, not just facts.** Add a predictive dimension to `SelfModelFact` (state → operation → expected state, with confidence), so metacognitive expectations have a generator instead of being hand-typed. This is Cox's Ω meeting Bongard's predictive self-model.
5. **Model the delegation seam for the mesh.** Add a `suspended` status to `Intention` and sketch a delegation pathway (goal → candidate reasoning about another node's skills → request). Gate it on user authority and keep workers at propose-only; the paper's theory-of-mind reasoning is fine to copy, the *execution* of delegation is not.
6. **Extend experiment 03 with the paper's fault class.** Its fault table covers stale self-model facts, contradictions, calibration, and misleading intentions. Add MIDCA's signature one: an *operator-level* wrong causal belief (a tool that silently does collateral damage, like spraying killing adjacent natives) and measure detection, repair efficacy, and recurrence — the MIDCA learning-agent vs standard-agent comparison, on Navi's own metrics.
7. **Doc nit**: `docs/ongoing-cognition.md` attributes the "metacognitive expectation" seam to "#6 Cox and Raja," but manifest #6 is this exact paper; the table's citation should point at this file.

## Risks / tensions

- **Metacognition is not free — and the paper says so itself.** It cites Conitzer on overhead and Norman on when introspection hurts. That is a direct endorsement of Navi-Brain's existing bounds: 30s path never invokes a model, high-brain work is single-flight and budgeted. Adopting the *formalism* does not license always-on self-inspection.
- **MIDCA autonomously rewrites its own action model** (FOIL modifies the spray operator in memory). Navi-Brain's invariants — no automatic factual-memory promotion, workers propose and never commit, user authority outranks persisted state — deliberately refuse that. The detection/explanation half of the paper transfers; the autonomous operator-revision half does not and should not.
- **Evidence is thin and domain-specific.** One symbolic grid domain, 2,000 trials, singleton meta-goal sets, very short meta-plans (the authors admit this). The claim "metacognition improves performance" is demonstrated for a toy planner, argued by analogy for richer cognition. Treat the empirical result as a proof of mechanism, not a scaling guarantee.
- **The paper's position that deliberate, goal-driven learning is metacognitive** deliberately conflicts with standard ML framing, and it sits awkwardly on top of an LLM substrate where most learning happens in weights or passive consolidation rather than via explicit learning goals. Navi-Brain should adopt the *explicit learning goal* idea without inheriting the anti-ML rhetorical baggage.
- **Anticipatory delegation via theory-of-mind** is the most socially loaded capability here: reasoning about another agent's goals and skills invites manipulation, and delegated goals cross an authority boundary. Keep it user-gated and proposal-only.

## Bottom line

Partially adopt. The paper is already the canonical source for Navi's stated metacognition aspirations — the gap is that the substrate verifies *world* outcomes (`ActionTrace`) but nothing verifies *mental* outcomes, and the self-model is a fact table, not a transition system. Adopt the metacognitive-expectation triple over cognitive operations and wire `ExecutiveInterrupt` into the loop; explicitly reject autonomous operator revision, which would break the no-automatic-promotion and workers-propose-only invariants. Most important consequence: the missing substrate for real metacognition is expectations over Navi's own cognitive phases, not better introspection prose.
