# 10 — pymdp

- **Paper**: pymdp: A Python library for active inference in discrete state spaces
- **Source**: DOI 10.21105/joss.04098, Journal of Open Source Software 7(73):4098, 2022 (JOSS software paper)
- **Local file**: `assets/papers/10-heins-et-al-2022-pymdp.pdf`

## Core idea

A software paper, not a theory paper: the first open-source Python package for simulating active inference agents on discrete POMDP generative models. The agent's world is a joint distribution P(o, s, u, φ) over observations, hidden states, control states, and hyperparameters, factored into categorical likelihoods and Dirichlet priors. Perception infers hidden states from observations; action is chosen by selecting policies that drive the hidden state toward preferred observations (expected free energy = pragmatic value + epistemic/information-gain value); learning updates the Dirichlet parameters. The library's design goals are the real content: modularity (call the Agent's methods in any order, subclass `step()`, swap inference algorithms like marginal message passing), an OpenAI-Gym-style Agent/Env loop that recursively exchanges observations and actions, and level-of-abstraction flexibility from a few lines of code down to bespoke variational inference. Positioned against DEM (SPM/MATLAB), which is monolithic and license-gated.

## Relevance to Navi-Brain

This is the paper behind `docs/literature-synthesis.md` §6 ("Uncertainty-aware action") — the repo already cites it for the lesson "represent uncertainty explicitly and select actions for both pragmatic value and information gain." What pymdp contributes to Navi-Brain specifically is a precise vocabulary for the exact seam the substrate already touches: Navi's `event -> intention -> retrieval -> action -> outcome -> discrepancy repair` loop is a *symbolic* perception-action cycle with prediction-error (`ActionTrace.expected` vs `observed`), preferred states (`Need` pressure ranges), and learning (`memory:consolidate`). pymdp shows what that loop looks like when the uncertainty is explicit and quantitative instead of text flags and fixed confidence numbers.

## Cross-reference to the existing system

**Already present:**
- **The perception-action cycle shape**: `src/Model/ActionTrace.php` — `expected`, `observed`, `match_status`, `repair_note` — is the discrete outcome-evaluation step of the cycle, driven by `action:start`/`action:finish` (`src/Core/ExecutiveCore.php:355`). pymdp's Agent/Env loop is the same closed loop; Navi-Brain's `pulse_30s`/`reflect_5m` heartbeats plus `desktop_presence`/`desktop_look` perception are the closed-loop machinery. The paper adds nothing structural here — it adds the probabilistic core Navi lacks.
- **Confidence as explicit uncertainty**: `Memory.confidence`, `SelfModelFact.confidence`, `ThoughtArtifact.confidence`, `Appraisal.uncertainty` — Navi already attaches numbers to beliefs. pymdp's point is these live on the wrong object: pymdp puts uncertainty in the generative model (beliefs about *hidden states given observations*), Navi puts it on facts and appraisals.
- **Epistemic value, seeded**: the `epistemic_novelty` need (`src/Core/ExecutiveCore.php:2216`, "seek unfamiliar evidence, alternative explanations, surprising connections") and `Appraisal.uncertainty` feeding `attention_score` (`ExecutiveCore.php:2127`) are Navi's information-gain drive. pymdp supplies the actual computation — EFE decomposes into a pragmatic term and an information-gain term — that the seed gestures at but never runs.
- **Preferred states**: pymdp agents maximize occupancy in preferred observations. `src/Model/Need.php` pressure variables with growth rates, thresholds, and trigger conditions are Navi's preferred-state set, expressed as homeostatic setpoints rather than observation priors. `docs/longitudinal-cognition-metrics.md` already carries the guardrail that homeostatic/active-inference control "must prove task benefit rather than assume that lower free energy, novelty, or pressure is intrinsically intelligent."
- **Learning as parameter update**: `memory:consolidate` (episode -> semantic with lineage) is Navi's learning-from-outcome path, functionally analogous to pymdp's Dirichlet hyperparameter updates after each trial — except Navi consolidates prose takeaways on a schedule, pymdp counts evidence into the model. Same impulse, different substrate.

**Partially present:**
- **Dual-value action selection**: the literature synthesis §6 already *declares* the intent ("select actions for both pragmatic value and information gain," governing "inspect, test, retrieve, ask, wait, or act"), and `Appraisal.uncertainty` + `epistemic_novelty` are the raw material. But nothing scores candidate next-actions with both terms. Navi's `Intention.next_action` is hand-authored, authority-gated text; the epistemic drive never shapes which action gets picked. The seed is real, the decision procedure is missing.
- **Precision**: pymdp's precision is the expected certainty of an observation channel — a *learned* quantity updated by outcomes. Navi's confidence values are assigned by model/human at write time and never reweighted by the `ActionTrace` match history sitting right there in the database. The evidence to learn reliability exists; the learning doesn't.

**Absent / open design:**
- **A generative model / belief state**: nothing in Navi-Brain computes a posterior over hidden states or factors P(o, s, u, φ). Retrieval is ranked lexical search (a documented boundary), and there is no probabilistic model of the world to do inference with. `docs/ongoing-cognition.md` may discuss predictive machinery for Navi's own cognition, but there is no world-model layer at all.
- **EFE / policy selection**: no computation over candidate policies balancing pragmatic and epistemic value. This is the paper's operational core and Navi's largest open gap, and it is also the piece that conflicts most with the safety invariants (below).
- **Dirichlet-style evidence accumulation**: consolidation is LLM text extraction, not counted observation -> parameter updates. Adopting pymdp wholesale would mean inventing a categorical generative model over an open-ended text/world environment, which the synthesis itself warns is brittle.

## Concrete implications for implementation

1. **Make `ActionTrace` outcomes confidence-weighted.** Add a `confidence`/`precision` field on `finishAction` (expected-strength and observation-reliability), and keep `match_status` as the hard verdict for audit. Then a small, read-only aggregation (per description kind or per intention context) can compute a running reliability — the seed of precision learning, costing nothing and violating no invariant.
2. **Give `epistemic_novelty` a decision role, proposal-only.** When a high-brain cycle or worker has candidate next-actions (or candidate retrievals), score them with two terms: a pragmatic one from the intention's `success_condition` and an epistemic one from `Appraisal.uncertainty` / retrieval novelty — then present the ranked result as *proposals*, never as autonomous execution. This converts §6 of the synthesis from aspiration into a bounded scoring function without adopting EFE as an execution driver.
3. **Learn reliability from the match history** rather than leaving `confidence` static: a `need:list`-style or appraisal-style signal ("action kind X has mismatched 3/5 times, drop its expected-confidence weight") — precision learning in Navi's pressure-variable idiom.
4. **Wire retrieval uncertainty into action selection.** `memory:search` is lexical; add the paper's epistemic insight cheaply by letting low-confidence search results (few/no matches) raise an "ask / inspect / test" bias in the bounded action choices §6 names, gated by the existing appraisal `uncertainty` field.
5. **Doc-level**: `docs/longitudinal-cognition-metrics.md` already has a "Learning progress" and "Curiosity advantage" metric family; anchor them to this paper explicitly and add a metric for *precision-learning* (does expected-confidence calibration improve with match history?) so the seed has a number to chase.

## Risks / tensions

- **EFE-driven action selection is the conflict.** Active inference agents choose actions autonomously to minimize expected free energy. Navi-Brain's invariants say actions are recorded, not autonomously executed, and user authority outranks persisted intentions. The transferable half is EFE as a *scoring/recommendation* function inside bounded workers; the non-transferable half is EFE as the thing that actually picks and runs actions. Adopt the former, explicitly name the latter off-limits.
- **POMDPs over open-ended environments get brittle.** The synthesis already says it: hand-specified discrete generative models stretch badly over Navi's real, text-and-tool world. This is a JOSS *software* paper — its evidence is the library's tests and modular API, not an empirical cognition claim. Borrow the mechanism vocabulary, don't import the finite-state ontology.
- **Active inference is a broad, contested church.** The paper itself is uncontroversial (tooling), but it silently inherits the FEP program's stronger claims about cognition. Navi-Brain's position — confidence on facts, evidence-backed claims, no free-energy *score of self* — is the right posture; nothing here should nudge toward FEP-as-universal-score.
- **Epistemic drives can chase trivia.** `epistemic_novelty` pressure, given a real information-gain term, could spiral into novelty-seeking for its own sake. The existing threshold/growth-rate governance on needs plus the metrics' "frontier diversity, not one-niche novelty" framing are the containment — keep them.

## Bottom line

Partially adopt — as a design pattern, not a library. The kernel worth taking is explicit, quantitative uncertainty on the action/outcome path (confidence-weighted outcomes + learned per-context reliability) and a bounded, proposal-only dual-value scorer (pragmatic + epistemic) for candidate actions, which is exactly what synthesis §6 already promises. The single most important consequence: turn `ActionTrace`'s hard matched/mismatched flag into a confidence-weighted observation and let match history learn precision — the cheapest faithful first step of active inference that costs nothing against the safety invariants, while rejecting EFE as an autonomous action-selection driver outright.
