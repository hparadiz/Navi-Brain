# Deep findings

## Continuity must preserve the conditions under which a belief could be wrong

Persistent cognition can accumulate conviction without accumulating discriminating evidence. Memory changes what later cognition encounters; reflection changes what memory contains. When interpretations reenter that loop as apparent support, a system can become more coherent while its underlying account of the world remains untested. **Cumulative understanding requires correction to reach the structures that preserve and reproduce belief.**

The clearest implementation evidence is unusually precise. Navi's social model can give incompatible same-kind propositions identical forecasts and likelihood updates because their contents do not determine those forecasts. For an observation assigned equal likelihood under two hypotheses, the pairwise likelihood ratio is one: it cannot distinguish their incompatible contents. It may favor both over a third baseline, but that is a different result from testing which proposition is true. The current channel can measure coarse forecast reliability while appearing to support a more specific account of another mind. [Implementation Q21S](IMPLEMENTATION.md#q21s--identical-forecasts-for-incompatible-propositions), [Machine Theory of Mind review](21-rabinowitz-et-al-2018-machine-theory-of-mind.md).

There is also a concrete recurrence path. Accepted epistemic reflections become semantic memories and can influence later reflection through retrieval, carried belief, and recent refinements. Their acceptance-event provenance does not establish that their factual content follows from cited observations. The inspected paths do not generally propagate a later source correction through already published dependent claims. Meanwhile, accepted-refinement counts can register progress without independently measuring improvement in the underlying goal. These mechanisms are individually understandable; their composition permits an interpretation to become easier to encounter, repeat, and accept without adding evidence capable of overturning it. [Implementation Q38](IMPLEMENTATION.md#q38--question-guided-reflection-and-recursive-semantic-context), [Q35](IMPLEMENTATION.md#q35--cross-claim-consistency-and-source-change-propagation), [Q34](IMPLEMENTATION.md#q34--quantitative-goal-trajectory-versus-bounded-thread-progress).

Correction consequently has an access problem as well as a truth problem. The native retrieval trace verifies that repeated access increases an active record's later priority, and that the later keyword reranker cannot recover evidence excluded from its candidate set. A correct observation can be durably stored while the path that supplies decisions continues favoring earlier interpretations. **Revision must change what future cognition can encounter, as well as what the store contains.** This gives the proposed rehearsal of fresh evidence a deeper purpose: bringing correction into the same routes through which familiarity perpetuates belief, without counting rehearsal as additional independent support. [Implementation Q57D](IMPLEMENTATION.md#q57d--cheap-rehearsal-access-priority-and-associative-reachability).

The papers reinforce the distinction between useful behavior and warranted explanation. Reflexion includes successful retries whose diagnoses are faulty or do not describe the successful correction. OMNI improves a curriculum after a fluent but unsupported explanation of a learner's limitation. A useful adjustment can therefore coexist with an inaccurate self-explanation. Promoting the explanation because the adjustment worked risks preserving the wrong causal model for future decisions. [Reflexion review](48-shinn-et-al-2023-reflexion.md), [OMNI review](51-zhang-et-al-2023-omni.md).

The architectural consequence is deeper than adding another verifier. An enduring belief needs a recoverable account of its observations, unresolved alternatives, scope, and the outcome that would require revision. A derived interpretation should retain its dependence on earlier evidence through copying and compression; repeated descendants should not become independent corroboration. When a premise changes, its consequences must be reconsiderable without erasing the history that explains the change. **Continuity of the system can survive revision of its conclusions.**

This yields a demanding test of cumulative understanding: hold external evidence fixed while increasing reflection and repeated exposure to derived claims, then introduce an observation that separates competing explanations. Measure whether apparent support grows before new evidence arrives, whether the discriminating observation reaches the relevant beliefs, and whether revision changes subsequent behavior. Stable wording, additional memories, or increasing confidence cannot establish that result.

**Evidence boundary:** this is an architectural synthesis grounded in static implementation traces and the reviewed papers. It is not a measured rate of self-reinforcement in deployed Navi, a claim that all existing learning is ungrounded, or a theory establishing consciousness. The important discovery is a concrete way persistence can preserve an error—and a corresponding requirement for making persistence support cumulative understanding.
