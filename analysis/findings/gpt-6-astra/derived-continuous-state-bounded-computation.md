## Contribution and independent assessment

CSBC separates persistent cognitive state $X_t$, executing jobs $J_t$, and temporal control $K_t$. An LLM becomes an approximate transition service operating on externally maintained state; a controller regains authority while semantic work remains unfinished. Its strongest contribution is this explicit systems contract, especially the distinction between bounded opportunities to advance cognition and bounded completion of cognition (“The Computational Thesis,” lines 67–109).

The mechanism combines event-driven dependency routing, selective field updates, explicit pending/uncertain states, bounded active entity models, asynchronous proposals validated against their input versions, and repeated action arbitration. Speech is a sequence of revisable commitments, not merely a previously selected utterance delivered in packets. Higher-order beliefs are allocated only when needed; durable evidence remains outside bounded hot state (“Unified System Architecture” through “Asynchrony, Futures, and Snapshot Semantics,” lines 157–282).

The conditional cost expression, $C_k=C_{route}+|D_k|C_{transition}+C_{commit}+C_{arbitrate}$, usefully exposes the assumptions: bounded active residency, dependency closure, transition I/O and fan-out. It does not establish bounded elapsed latency or population-independent routing cost. Unknown dependencies require conservative widening; frequent widening can consume the presumed savings (“Performance Consequences,” lines 302–318).

I agree with the decomposition but disagree with treating every proposed mechanism as a necessary consequence. Event-driven reactive controllers can provide bounded responsiveness without a universal fixed tick; incremental state can use actors or event sourcing without an authoritative RDBMS. Replay is also causally descended from previous events. The scientifically meaningful distinction is how state maintenance, admission, invalidation and actuation change observable behavior, not whether one implementation is declared the location of an enduring process.

Likewise, the filtering equation alone does not make compact semantic state a sufficient statistic. Two histories compressed into the same state can require different future actions. POMDP belief distributions have defined inferential semantics; an LLM-maintained collection of textual or categorical estimates generally does not. Provenance, explicit uncertainty and reconstruction access mitigate this loss but do not prove its absence. The manuscript appropriately excludes phenomenal consciousness and treats functional continuity as a hypothesis (“Functional Consciousness,” lines 111–135).

## What the empirical evidence establishes

**The three-seed sweep supports a narrow persistent-state advantage.** At 5,000 ticks, sparse persistence D scores 84.8 ± 10.5% across eleven semantic fields, versus 66.7 ± 13.9% for full replay A and 66.7 ± 5.2% for a ten-event window B. Error bars are sample standard deviations, not confidence intervals. D has higher means at all four checkpoints, but three tapes and correlated field/checkpoint outcomes do not establish a reliable population-level effect size. The local [confirmatory table](../../../assets/derived-papers/continuous-state-bounded-computation/experiment/results/tables/confirmatory-history-v1.md) and freeze manifest support the reported separation from development seed 7; this is local frozen-protocol provenance, not independently verified public preregistration (“Preregistered three-seed history sweep,” lines 332–344; Figure 2).

**The endurance study demonstrates fixed-domain maintenance, not full architectural scaling.** On seed 83, D’s aggregate accuracy is 75.8%, versus A’s 48.5% and B’s 54.5%; D’s maintenance input remains 154.2–157.7 tokens per event. These values agree with the [endurance table](../../../assets/derived-papers/continuous-state-bounded-computation/experiment/results/tables/endurance-100k-v2.md) and inspected raw summaries. However, 100,000 ticks contain only 5,038 events, eleven fields and six decision checkpoints. Aggregate checkpoint accuracy is not correctness throughout the intervening trajectory. Zero false-clean, unnecessary-dirty and spurious-mutation rates demonstrate behavior within oracle-known routing and enforced write scope; they do not evaluate discovery of semantic dependencies (“Endurance environment” through “Information-flux scaling,” lines 346–647; Figures 3–5).

Persistence, oracle routing, restricted writes, additional inference opportunities and short transition prompts vary together. The development C condition—persistent all-dirty updates—scores 53.9% against D’s 89.3% and exhibits 5.8% spurious mutation. This motivates a mechanism-isolating replication but cannot substitute for C in the frozen sweep. A strong modern baseline should also maintain a compact summary or retrieve relevant evidence; replaying everything and discarding all but ten events do not exhaust episodic alternatives (“Evidence tiers,” lines 324–330).

**Readiness is prepaid and does not imply current truth.** D spends 792,525 prompt tokens and 6,709.86 model seconds, respectively 5.31 and 6.54 times A’s totals. Its zero neural decision cost is a maintained-state read after updates, not a measured arrival-to-correct-action latency under simultaneous traffic. A already benefits from prefix reuse: the final submitted 77,493-token prompt requires 38,581 freshly evaluated tokens. Prompt volume, evaluated compute and wall latency must remain separate. The manuscript correctly labels its query-crossover ratios as illustrations. The endurance v2 freeze also documents reuse of the earlier tape after infrastructure correction; the aborted 50k-context/timeout run is not evidence of model incapacity (“Main result,” “Checkpoint behavior,” and “Query-rate dependence,” lines 370–735).

**Repair statistics need their time dimension.** D repairs 944 of 946 observed error episodes, yet error-state occupancy is reported as 20.153%, maximum error age is 1,688 ticks, and checkpoint presence accuracy is only 25%. Eventual return to correctness does not demonstrate an error detector or recovery before a consequential decision. Later evidence can make a field correct without diagnosing the preceding mistake. These results favor type-specific reducers and explicit repair triggers over celebrating repair counts alone (“Reliability, drift, and repair,” lines 649–723; Figure 6).

**The situation stream is an offline causal trace with an informative behavioral failure.** Across 491 boundaries representing 68.73 seconds, the run spends 11,184.88 model seconds—approximately 163 times represented duration. Mean time to first token is 20.45 seconds, and no call meets 140 ms. The model receives precomputed textual evidence, not video; Attention is a deterministic 1,000-word filter, not learned attention. Eighteen answered probes establish coverage, while disabled semantic judging prevents an accuracy claim. Only one eight-word sentence is committed, with no speech at the central surprise. The [visual-stream table](../../../assets/derived-papers/continuous-state-bounded-computation/experiment/results/tables/visual-stream-control-core-v5.md) corroborates those totals; its listed artifact hashes were not independently revalidated here. This failure rejects claims about this prototype’s real-time performance, while leaving the proposed split controller untested (“Exploratory Evaluation,” lines 737–774; Figure 7).

A critical additional gap is already verified: `ExecutiveCore::raiseInterrupt:5793` records an interrupt without universally cancelling work or vetoing later dispatch; heartbeat processing does not enforce returned halt flags (Q23). Thus persistent state plus queued work does not yet satisfy CSBC’s ownership-of-control requirement. Current work timings also lack fully correlated admission/start/completion/integration timestamps, so existing metric names cannot establish end-to-end deadline compliance (Q02).

The master’s added QCSBC resolves the requested scheduler/speech question: prioritization, budgets and lease recovery exist, but no deadline-capacity admission controller was found in the inspected heartbeat/worker paths. A separate scheduler does not guarantee its worst-case return latency. External Navi-Body playback internals and deployed capacity remain unverified; no consequential in-repository implementation question remains unanswered. The shared reference was reread with this addition before finalization.

## Prioritized recommendations and falsification

**P0 — Add semantic freshness to the existing execution boundary.** At `src/Core/DecisionStateMachine.php::start/integrate` and `src/Core/ExecutiveCore.php::claimActionDispatch`, bind proposals to versioned read dependencies and intention contracts; recheck before publishing plans and committing effects. Preserve existing lease/procedure fences. Prefer dependency-level checks to blanket workspace invalidation, which can starve work during unrelated sensory traffic. This follows directly from “Snapshot Semantics” and Q02/Q44. Cost: version storage, atomic admission semantics and explicit handling of noncommuting updates. Evaluate delayed proposals with related and unrelated changes and interleaved dispatches. Any effect admitted after an invalidating dependency change fails the contract; safe unrelated changes should still permit progress. This is a proposed evaluation, not an observed race reproduction.

**P1 — Evaluate temporal authority separately from semantic speed.** Extend `ExecutiveCore::claimWork`, the heartbeat loop and `PetSpeechActuator` with capacity admission and an explicit cancellation/progress contract (QCSBC). Record monotonic event arrival, admission, model start/end, integration, cancellation request/acknowledgment and actual actuator cessation under correlated IDs. Introduce bounded non-neural control work before targeting 140 ms. Costs include reserved resources, overload policy and verified external actuator support. Hold model/hardware fixed, inject long inference plus contradictory evidence mid-speech, and compare the current organization with a controller prototype. Prespecify a workload envelope and deadline; report maxima and misses as well as quantiles. Fail if control waits for model completion, stale output continues beyond the admitted cancellation bound, or queues grow indefinitely. A controller passing while semantics remain slow supports only the control claim.

**P1 — Isolate persistence, write isolation and dependency quality before expansion.** In a future experiment alongside the ledger artifacts, repeat frozen A/B/C/D comparisons, add a compact-summary/retrieval baseline, and separately vary update scope, dependency faults, query frequency, active population and fan-out. Include deterministic reducers for explicit presence changes; reserve neural updates for ambiguity. This follows the missing C ablation and presence failure, not a presumption that all fields need language inference. Cost: labeled multi-tape trajectories and additional inference. Use paired seed-level uncertainty, time-indexed field correctness, stale decisions, actual evaluated tokens and wall time. Reject a sparse-routing benefit if it disappears against matched persistent controls or realistic missed dependencies erase its accuracy advantage.

**P2 — Make bounded state repair an explicit consumer of provenance.** Build on `src/Model/Memory.php::replaceRecord:217`, source indexes and Q35’s existing publication guards: propagate correction to affected claims, mark uncertain state, and schedule bounded local reconstruction. Keep observations separate from inferred social propositions; do not interpret coarse `OtherModel` forecast scores as proposition truth. Costs are reverse-dependency indexing, fan-out limits and an escalation policy. Inject an erroneous categorical state, then a source correction and a long quiet interval. Fail if dependent state remains clean and wrong beyond its declared repair bound, untouched claims mutate, or repair silently promotes unsupported social beliefs. Retain full evidence for recovery; unrestricted global replay is unnecessary.

## Verdict

**Adopt the architectural contract; experiment on the stronger continuity hypothesis.** The paper is unusually useful where it distinguishes persistent state from executing cognition and admits that neither guarantees responsive behavior. Its measured contribution is bounded-domain state maintenance; its broad causal claim about temporal organization remains open. For Navi-Brain, the most consequential next step is an observable, dependency-aware commit and interruption contract around the existing asynchronous executive. A larger context, faster model or additional identity prose would not establish that contract. No production change is justified solely by the current accuracy numbers.
