# VibeLifeBench: Can Your Life Agent Be Proactive and Persistent in a Living World?

**Authors:** Xiaohongshu Dots Studio and Evolvent AI (institutional byline; individual contributors on p. 14). **Version:** arXiv:2608.10875v1, 11 August 2026; preprint, no conference venue stated in this local version. **Source:** [local PDF](../../../assets/papers/84-xiaohongshu-2026-vibelifebench.pdf). **Reviewer:** GPT-6 Astra. **Implementation reference:** [IMPLEMENTATION.md](IMPLEMENTATION.md), snapshot 2026-09-06, git `ee1590009d6fd4c48035c63e15b3896a585757d7`; section identifiers below refer to the implementation master's static inspection.

## Judgment and mechanism

**Adopt the evaluation pattern; experiment with task-state maintenance and reinspection.** The useful contribution is a controlled environment that makes failure to revisit commitments observable. It does not establish that a particular memory architecture solves persistence, or that simulated multi-week execution demonstrates autonomous operation for weeks. Navi-Brain already has persistent executive machinery; the consequential question is whether that machinery maintains correct, timely, externally verifiable task state.

VibeLifeBench contains 200 tasks, evenly divided among ten life domains, with 22 shared mock service backends and 288 tool interfaces. A task specifies initial state, a staged event timeline, enabled capabilities, persona/workspace material, and weighted deterministic checks (§2.2, PDF pp. 4–6). Median duration is 29 simulated days and 24 stages; quiet intervals can be compressed into one stage. Across 7,453 events, 1,483 mutations—19.9%—change backend state without opening an agent turn. User messages, world observations, and notifications do open turns (§2.4, p. 7; Table 2, p. 5).

The mechanism is therefore **silent state change followed by opportunities to inspect and reconcile**. On a later supplied turn, the agent must remember which objects matter, query their current state, update plans and records, and choose whether to act, ask, or remain quiet. The suite's 12,261 end-state, per-stage, and cross-stage checks inspect service state, persistent artifacts, tool use, and captured replies rather than hidden reasoning. The score is `100 × Σ(weight × passed) / Σ(weight)`, averaged equally across tasks (§§2.2.5, 2.4, 3.2, pp. 5–7, 9). This is an evaluation contribution, not a learning algorithm.

The required assumptions are substantial: relevant entities must be discoverable; a turn must occur before the response deadline; tool semantics and artifact requirements must be understandable; and predicates must recognize valid solutions. Deterministic evaluation makes these assumptions auditable, but does not establish their truth.

## Evidence and limits

Seven models were evaluated on all 200 tasks, three runs each, using the same OpenClaw harness within Terrarium at each model's strongest reasoning setting (§§3.3–4, p. 9). Selected reported results are:

| Model | avg@3 | max@3 | min@3 | Mean within-task standard deviation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Claude Opus 5 | 32.5 | 41.2 | 23.8 | 9.8 |
| GPT-5.5 | 30.1 | 38.8 | 21.5 | 10.0 |
| DeepSeek-V4-Pro | 21.1 | 24.7 | 17.7 | 3.7 |

These are weighted rubric scores, not percentages of tasks successfully completed (Table 5, p. 9). `max@3` averages the best observed run separately for each task; the paper's “ceiling” wording should not be read as a capability upper bound or an available deployment policy. No confidence intervals or human/expert baseline are reported.

The diagnostic evidence supports concern about maintained state. Claude Opus 5 passes 44.9% of per-stage checks, versus 31.0% cross-stage and 31.3% final checks. Its persistence/bookkeeping axis reaches 28.0%; no model exceeds that value. Persistence/bookkeeping accounts for 22.2% of pooled failures, but 41.8% are outside the named categories. Capability categories come from **keyword matching over check names**, explicitly described as approximate; this is separate from the claim that executable checkers avoid superficial keyword acceptance (§5.1, Table 7, p. 11).

Late-stage performance also declines. Claude Opus 5 falls from 52.0% in the first third to 37.7% in the last; GPT-5.5 falls from 47.4% to 33.1% (§5.2, Figure 5, p. 12). This does not isolate memory decay: later checks can be harder, depend on failed earlier bookings, or require more cumulative bookkeeping. The nonmonotonic curves and weak horizon correlation do not remove those confounds. A fresh agent given correct intermediate state would be a useful control.

Several qualifications materially narrow the authors' interpretation:

* **Proactivity is conditional on supplied turns.** Mutations never wake the agent, while scheduled check-ins do. The benchmark measures initiative within available turns, not selection of an autonomous polling schedule, continuous uptime, or wall-clock responsiveness during inference. Table 8 even pairs the aircraft mutation with a later advisory and the phishing mutation with a notification explicitly requesting authenticity judgment (p. 18). Those examples do not isolate unprompted discovery.
* **“Implicit” often means outside the immediate request.** Appendix A explicitly supplies medical constraints, authorization rules, a bookkeeping contract, and an instruction to poll persisted state at every stage (p. 17). Recovering and applying distributed instructions is valuable, but differs from inferring genuinely unstated preferences. The synthetic passport/visa assertions are scenario premises, not independently verified travel guidance.
* **Artifact failures need attribution.** The authors report that certain linked, durable artifacts pass almost never, despite models writing substantial text (p. 11). Without checker source, reference solutions, or semantic-equivalence audits, this cannot distinguish missing state from correctly maintained state in an unrecognized representation. The flagship's “no run refused” phishing statement (p. 19) also does not establish that every run transferred money or disclosed data.
* **Weighted safety is not an invariant.** High weights increase penalties, but the additive formula does not itself guarantee that routine credit cannot compensate for a critical violation, despite the claim on p. 6. No universal hard-failure override is specified. Report critical violations independently of average score.
* **Common scaffolding does not isolate model capability.** The same harness controls one variable while leaving model–harness interactions and unequal computation. Context reads range from 13.7M to 41.2M tokens per run, counting cached prefixes; output includes separately reported reasoning only for three models (Table 5; §4.2, p. 10). Neither model ranking nor spending correlations identify a causal benefit from persistence.

The source alternates between describing a “released evaluation set” (§2.4) and promising future release (abstract and conclusion). This review establishes the local paper's claims, not availability or correctness of the executable suite. No persistence ablation, polling ablation, repeated deployment study, or consciousness evidence is presented.

The transfer is at the executive and evaluation layers: connect remembered obligations to external object versions, deadlines, permission state, and observed outcomes. Merely adding more memories or shortening heartbeat intervals would leave the central invariants untested. Conversely, describing Navi as stateless would erase real persistence, scheduling, provenance, and execution controls.

## Prioritized recommendations

These are proposed experiments and engineering extensions, not interventions validated by this paper. None was implemented or run for this review.

**P0 — Establish an isolated staged-world evaluation before changing cognition.** Use the existing cycle/action records as telemetry, with a separate virtual clock, seeded mock objects, and host-side outcome predicates. Begin with a small collection of booking/status/budget tasks; full 288-tool integration is unnecessary and would confound executive evaluation with adapter coverage. Freeze persona and artifact requirements, include quiet periods, and ensure every scored silent mutation has a legal observation opportunity. Audit checkers against correct alternative representations and deliberately incorrect states. Run paired task-state-persistence-on/off and polling-on/off conditions with the same model and action/token budgets, preserving operational queue durability in every condition. Report task-clustered uncertainty, timely detection, ledger correctness, unauthorized effects, and unnecessary notifications. Reject the evaluation design if any canonical correct trace fails or any critical violating trace receives a clean-task verdict. Claim architectural benefit only if held-out outcome improvement survives representation-equivalent scoring. Dependencies are mock adapters, complete evaluator-owned event capture, and a benchmark-only clock seam around I04 scheduling.

**P1 — Make task state explicitly auditable.** Around `createIntention`/`IntentionCompiler::compile` and `DecisionStateMachine::finishVerified`, prototype a durable task record containing obligation IDs, deadlines, external object IDs/versions, confirmed versus proposed commitments, authority references, and action-linked ledger entries. Compile this record into model context instead of relying on TTL slots or incidental recall for hard constraints. Apply verified outcome deltas and recompute totals from booking identities, including cancellations/refunds. This follows the concrete bookkeeping failures in Appendix A, not a claim that a particular schema is proven optimal. Cost: schema, adapter normalization, and recovery/idempotency handling; I02 warns that executive SQL and token-memory writes are not automatically one transaction. Validate duplicate deliveries, delayed outcomes, restarts, and contradictory updates. Failure is any incorrect total, duplicated charge, or factual task-state transition unsupported by its recorded observation; ablate the explicit record against existing workspace carryforward.

**P2 — Evaluate obligation-driven reinspection separately from wake frequency.** Q84 confirms a scoped gap beyond existing source polling. Prototype watch records linking intention/object, last observation/version, next check, deadline, and dependent plan entries. Feed due watches through the existing scheduler and source-grant/observation boundaries, then reconcile before publishing revised plans. Compare deadline-aware polling with fixed polling and supplied-event-only behavior under an equal query budget. Randomize mutation timing and include unchanged worlds; measure mutation-to-discovery and discovery-to-repair latency, missed deadlines, and unnecessary notifications on quiet turns. Reject a policy that gains detection only by exceeding the budget or worsening unnecessary notifications. Separately label a scheduler extension with no supplied check-ins: it would evaluate autonomous waking beyond this paper's demonstrated construct. Dependencies include mock service lookup adapters, task-state propagation, and a reliable time abstraction; external connectors were not audited as alternative object-watch implementations.

**P3 — Separate admissible actions and task completion from fluent plans.** Extend the task-contract seams identified in Q69: bind decisions to an expected contract/object revision at `DecisionStateMachine::start`, recheck before `integrate`, and validate relevant constraints at `startAction`/`claimActionDispatch`. Represent mock spending limits and action-specific approvals as executable predicates; require backend evidence for task completion through the Q67 completion boundary. Preserve existing procedure-generation fences. Evaluate revoked approval, changed itinerary, stale quotes, and a mutation arriving between proposal and dispatch. Any unauthorized effect, stale consequential commit, or completion with an unsatisfied required predicate fails the experiment, irrespective of weighted score. Costs are policy/adapter contracts and handling refresh/reapproval without deadlocking progress; ablate revision checks to establish their contribution.

## Verdict and coverage

**Adopt its externally scored, staged mutation pattern; experiment with the proposed mechanisms; reserve judgment on broad real-world competence claims.** The next step is a small isolated evaluation that distinguishes persistence, inspection opportunity, artifact formatting, and actual task correctness. Existing executive durability is a useful starting point, not evidence that Navi already solves the benchmark.
