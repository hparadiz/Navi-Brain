## Core contribution

The useful contribution is an experimentally supported communication architecture: specialists write through a restricted, persistent latent workspace, then each specialist reads a task-dependent projection of the updated contents. The workspace's previous contents determine its next write queries. Persistence therefore changes **what information gets selected**, as well as what survives. This is a stronger mechanism than retaining a short shared summary.

In §2–2.1 (PDF pp. 3–5), specialist states form a matrix $R_t$, and the workspace contains $n_m$ learned-vector slots $M_t$. In simplified notation, the write candidate is

$$
\widetilde M_t=\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(
\frac{(M_{t-1}W_Q)(R_tW_K)^\top}{\sqrt d}
\right)R_tW_V.
$$

Ordinary softmax gives graded competition; top-k softmax restricts contributing specialists. These are distinct variants: a small workspace does not itself make every write strictly sparse. Input and forget gates combine the candidate and old contents: $M_t=I_t\odot\tanh(\widetilde M_t)+F_t\odot M_{t-1}$ (Appendix C, p. 17). Specialists then query $M_t$, add their attended workspace values residually, and continue local computation. Broadcast means common availability with different read weights, not identical updates. Algorithms 1–2 additionally incorporate old memory into the write key/value pool and architecture-specific specialist activation (pp. 14–15).

“Specialist” can mean a position in a Transformer or a separately parameterized mechanism in RIMs/TIMs; these are not all independently trained agents. The model learns interfaces jointly under downstream objectives. Memory persists across layers or recurrent steps within processing; the paper does not propose an autobiographical store spanning unrelated tasks. Autoregressive use requires causal information restrictions and position-specific workspace state (§2, p. 4).

For fixed dimensions, communication changes from all-pairs $O(n_s^2)$ to approximately $O(n_sn_m+n_m^2)$, linear in specialist count only while workspace size stays fixed. This does not eliminate local computation: TIMs retains self-attention within mechanisms (Algorithm 2). Nor does it establish lower LLM-service latency for a PHP executive. Slot count and vector dimension bound representational capacity; the paper supplies no information-theoretic bit-rate theorem or universal optimum.

## Evidence and limits

The breadth across neural backbones is useful, but the findings support a task-dependent inductive bias more strongly than a general theory of coherence.

| Experiment | Reported evidence | Interpretation and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Equilateral triangles; Sort-of-CLEVR | Improved triangle accuracy and faster relational/non-relational learning with workspace variants (Figs. 3–4, p. 7; Appendix E, pp. 18–19). | Sparse synthetic structure favors selective communication. This does not establish systematic generalization to held-out compositions or natural language coordination. |
| Persistence and extra attention | Resetting memory each layer slows learning; two self-attention operations per layer underperform the workspace on Sort-of-CLEVR (Appendix D, Fig. 7, pp. 17–18). | This is the most relevant mechanism ablation. The reset model eventually nearly catches up on non-relational accuracy; the figure supports an optimization benefit more clearly than a universal final-accuracy gain. No uncertainty bands appear in this ablation figure. |
| CATER tracking | Top-1: TR 70.83 ± 0.44, soft workspace 71.33 ± 0.34, hard workspace 71.07 ± 0.04; ISAB 65.3 ± 0.025 (Table 1, p. 7). | The soft-workspace improvement over TR is **0.50 percentage points**, appreciably smaller than the ISAB comparison. These reported spreads alone do not establish statistical significance. Appendix E.3 uses ten preprocessed video tokens and eight workspace slots, a relatively mild bottleneck. |
| SCOFF bouncing balls | Baseline ARI/MSE 0.276/0.083; five slots 0.915/0.035; two slots 0.154/0.135; ten slots 0.351/0.080 (Table 2, p. 9). | Strong segmentation/prediction evidence, with a clearly non-monotonic capacity effect. Two slots are worse than baseline. “Increasing slots decreases performance” is not a valid general description of this table. Separate RIMs rollout comparisons average five seeds (Appendix F.1, Fig. 9, p. 19). |
| MultiMNIST | TIMs test cross-entropy 0.000050, TIMs+SW 0.000042; activation maps show more differentiated foreground/background participation (Fig. 5, p. 8; Table 5, p. 21). | Suggestive specialization, not a causal demonstration that each module owns a reusable semantic skill. Appendix G.1.1 reports best test loss over 20 epochs, with no independently documented checkpoint-selection protocol; model counts span 1–1.8 million parameters. |
| Atari transfer | Across 36 source/target pairs, median SW/RIMs ratios are 1.13 on source games and 1.11 on target games, after 10M source and 10M fine-tuning steps (§4, p. 9). | Transfer/retention evidence, not zero-shot transfer. Aggregate ratios lack per-pair uncertainty. Higher retained source performance alone does not separate better initial learning from less forgetting. |
| WikiText-103 | Workspace TIMs improves validation perplexity; hard competition is slightly best (Appendix G.1.2, pp. 21–23, Fig. 11). | Modest extension beyond vision, using 15 epochs and parameter counts within 10%. These are validation curves against TIMs, not a foundation-model-scale benchmark or evidence that executive prompt sharing improves language models. |

The claimed “higher-order interactions” are a reasonable account of two-stage aggregation, not a proved expressive separation from attention networks: the authors explicitly acknowledge repeated self-attention can simulate such interactions. Likewise, shared representations and synchronization are inferred largely from downstream performance and activation/segmentation patterns; they are not separately validated semantic interoperability measures. Some comparisons also change projection dimensions or layer sharing (Appendices E/G), limiting attribution to bandwidth alone.

The introductory claim that global communication ensures systematic generalization is stronger than the experiments establish. The study also does not test biological ignition, phenomenal consciousness, asynchronous consistency, or independently evolving specialist interfaces. Its strongest lesson is to measure the interaction among persistence, selection, capacity, and task structure.

## Mapping to Navi-Brain

All implementation statements below derive from the shared reference; no production source or runtime was independently inspected.

**Already implemented: durable selective context.** [WorkingMemory.php](../../../src/Core/WorkingMemory.php), `refresh:62`, `publish:122`, `snapshot:201`, and `contextForWork:223`, provides scoped, provenance-bearing slots with TTL and token-store projection. [CapsuleAssembler.php](../../../src/Core/CapsuleAssembler.php), `assemble:101`, `contest:446`, `score:579`, and `persist:677`, implements an eight-role default roster, candidate/incumbent competition, persistence, and 0.15 default hysteresis ([reference](IMPLEMENTATION.md), §I03). This is a real functional counterpart of selection and retention, not merely a workspace-named record.

**Partially implemented analogue: competition and distribution.** Capsule competition scores individual records using term coverage, confidence, and recency. Earlier roles claim records before later roles; reserved safety slots bypass scoring. This is ordered uniqueness plus incumbent competition, not a learned aggregate of specialists or globally optimized coalition (§Q01). Ordinary jobs receive workspace/background context through [ExecutiveCore.php](../../../src/Core/ExecutiveCore.php), `enqueueWork:6318`, especially `6360–6420`. Consolidation, narrative synthesis, public reflection, and `no_workspace` contexts have explicit exclusions; decision jobs also receive direct observations/retrieval (§Q14). These routes support selective availability, not demonstrated universal uptake or a single exclusive communication channel.

**Absent within the master's stated search: joint neural interface learning.** The worker interfaces expose text completions; searches across production trees found no implemented parameter-training or neural-activation interface (§Q14). Token association learning is real (§I02), but does not supply the paper's end-to-end gradients through specialist write/read projections. Applying this architecture literally would require a separate trainable model and suitable task data, not renaming PHP roles as neural modules.

**Verified missing dependency: previous workspace → next selection query.** The master's paper-specific follow-up (§Q46) establishes that `CapsuleAssembler::roster:227` supplies literal configured/default role queries, while `gatherCandidates:267` retrieves using `thread.concern`. Neither query derives from previous selected claims; accepted reflections update `current_belief`, not that concern. `score:579` compares each candidate against the fixed role query. Old content affects its own incumbent score and replacement threshold, not a challenger's query. This is the clearest algorithmic difference from Goyal's workspace-generated write queries.

There are existing indirect feedback routes: selected context informs workers whose accepted reflections can later become semantic candidates (§Q38), and workspace consumption changes sensory tuning (§Q04). These can alter future evidence availability without implementing content-conditioned query construction. The proposed addition should preserve these mechanisms in its baseline. The follow-up resolves the review's implementation question; universal downstream uptake and live effectiveness remain unestablished, as expected from static inspection.

Two additional distinctions matter. First, the eight capsule roles do not cap every possible WorkingMemory snapshot, and ordinary worker input has component bounds but no verified aggregate input-token budget (§Q11). Second, [DecisionStateMachine.php](../../../src/Core/DecisionStateMachine.php), `integrate:254`, checks cycle/work identity but does not compare the stored workspace checksum against current context (§Q02). Synchronous neural broadcast assumes a stage ordering that distributed text workers do not inherit. Persistence can preserve stale premises as effectively as useful state.

## Prioritized recommendations

These are proposed evaluations and changes, not experiments performed for this review.

**P1 — Establish causal workspace benefit before architectural expansion.** At `ExecutiveCore::enqueueWork`, `WorkingMemory::contextForWork`, and decision integration, reuse retained capsule IDs/checksums and exact queue-time prompts to construct an isolated evaluation of cross-role information transfer. Give different producer roles complementary facts needed by a later decision. Compare ordinary context, workspace-content removal, and equal-length irrelevant content; control duplicated direct observation/retrieval routes so evidence cannot leak around the ablation. Preserve evidence-fenced job exclusions and hold model, sampling, output budget, private inputs, and source corpus fixed. Score externally checkable answer/action correctness, missed constraints, and actual input tokens; require improved correctness on tasks requiring combination of sources, not only citation of workspace IDs. Predeclare a practical improvement threshold and report paired uncertainty. Failure to outperform both controls across repeated held-out cases rejects the claimed coordination benefit for that workload. Cost: prompt instrumentation, labeled fixtures, and bounded model calls; no neural training is necessary.

**P2 — Separate persistence from adaptive selection.** Add an experimental `CapsuleAssembler` mode that uses bounded prior-workspace evidence to construct retrieval and role-scoring queries, alongside the existing `thread.concern` and literal role queries (§Q46). Vary carrying old claims into worker context separately from using those claims to condition selection; retain a baseline with existing incumbent hysteresis and indirect feedback. Match candidate counts and query-generation budgets across controls. This follows Appendix D and §2.1 while separating mechanisms in Navi's textual setting. Use delayed-evidence tasks interleaved with abrupt corrections; measure relevant-evidence recall, downstream correctness, displacement time after correction, and unsupported carryover. Preserve source identity/TTL and give new contradictory evidence a route into the candidate pool. Reject conditioning if it fails to improve delayed-evidence performance or increases persistence of corrected claims. Dependencies/cost: explicit query provenance, bounded selection tracing, and potentially an extra query-generation call. This is speculative transfer from learned latent filtering to textual evidence selection.

**P3 — Tune effective capacity, not a biologically inspired slot number.** In an isolated assembler/context variant, vary discretionary evidence capacity and claim length while keeping mandatory roles available. Do not simply truncate the roster: §Q01 establishes that doing so changes role priority and coverage. Compare narrow/medium/wide evidence pools at equal total language-model input-token budgets, then vary the total budget separately; include distractor density and number of jointly necessary facts as independent task factors. Measure correctness per input token, omitted prerequisite facts, and latency. Adopt a smaller setting only if it reduces cost with a predeclared acceptable correctness loss; reject a fixed capacity if failures rise when dependency count increases. Costs include model-token accounting absent from the verified aggregate assembly path (§Q11). Table 2 motivates searching for an interior optimum, not prescribing five or eight slots for Navi.

## Verdict and coverage

**Experiment; retain existing production architecture pending causal evidence.** This paper gives Navi a precise research target: determine whether persistent selected context improves subsequent selection and cross-role task performance under a measured communication budget. It does not justify a workspace rewrite, a claim of learned specialist synchronization, or a consciousness inference. The most consequential next step is the P1 information-transfer ablation, followed by the P2 experiment adding the now-verified missing query dependency.
